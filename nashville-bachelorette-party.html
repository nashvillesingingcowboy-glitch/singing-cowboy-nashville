The Concept

Every bachelorette hits Broadway.
Only yours gets a song first.

You're getting ready at the hotel or Airbnb. The bride thinks the night is just starting. Then a cowboy walks in with a guitar — and starts singing about her. The night she met Jake. The nickname from college. The time she danced on the table in Destin. All of it.

Here's how the bridesmaids make it happen:

  • 🔒 You book the Cowboy privately — the bride never sees the booking
  • 📝 You secretly send the stories: how they met, inside jokes, nicknames, the fiancé's best and worst moments
  • 🎸 The Cowboy writes an original song about the bride & future groom
  • 😱 He shows up at your hotel, Airbnb or rooftop and performs it live — right before you head to Broadway
  • 📸 Photos and video after, while she's still processing what just happened

What Goes in the Song

Idea starters for the dirt

Not sure what to send? The best songs come from the stuff she'd never expect to hear in public:

🔥 Song material ideas (examples — not real client stories)

  • How she met her fiancé — the real version, not the family version
  • The nickname that's been banned since college
  • The dating disaster her friends still quote
  • What she orders after two margaritas
  • His terrible dancing (or her terrible singing)
  • The group chat's favorite inside joke
  • Anything she'd kill you for telling — perfect

Don't remember exactly how she met her future husband? That's okay. Give the Cowboy what you know. He has absolutely no problem making up the rest.
