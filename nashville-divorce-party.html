What Goes in the Song

The kind of material that makes a divorce song great

🔥 Ex material ideas (examples — not real client stories)

  • The ex's first name (last names stay out of it)
  • Why it ended — the version she tells her friends
  • His best/worst qualities, ranked
  • The thing he did that made everyone groan
  • Her favorite "I can't believe I married him" story
  • Anything she wants sung loudly in public
  • Things the Cowboy should NOT mention (ex: kids, legal stuff)

The Cowboy keeps it funny and respectful of the things that matter. This is about celebrating her — not airing anything that could cause real trouble.
