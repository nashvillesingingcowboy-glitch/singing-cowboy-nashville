Partner FAQ
How does wholesale pricing work?
Partners receive pricing below public retail on every package. You mark the experience up to whatever you want to charge your clients and keep the difference. Ask for the partner rate card when you reach out.
How does the referral commission work?
You send clients to Singing Cowboy Nashville. When a referral books and completes a performance, you earn a commission. Simple and recurring — referrals keep coming every season.
Can you work with our hotel or venue directly?
Yes. We regularly perform in hotel lobbies, suites, rooftops and venue spaces. We coordinate directly with your team on timing, entrance and location.
Do you have materials we can use?
Yes — we can provide copy, photos and a simple way for your clients to book and submit their details. Just ask when you sign up.
