FAQ

Questions, answered

What is Singing Cowboy Nashville?
A surprise entertainment experience: a real Nashville musician shows up in a cowboy hat with an acoustic guitar and performs live for your group — including original personalized songs written from stories her friends secretly provide.
Where will the Cowboy perform?
Hotels, Airbnbs, rooftops, private venues and backyards — anywhere in the Nashville area where a surprise performance works. We'll coordinate the exact spot when you book.
Can you surprise someone at our hotel?
Yes — hotel lobbies, suites and common areas are some of our most popular surprise locations. We'll work with your group to time the entrance perfectly.
Do you perform at Airbnbs?
Absolutely. Airbnb and vacation rental surprises are a specialty — the group is getting ready, the Cowboy shows up, the room goes quiet, the song starts.
How does the personalized song work?
Friends secretly send stories, jokes and details about the guest of honor. The Cowboy writes an original song using that material and performs it live. She'll recognize herself in every verse — and the group will never let her forget it.
What information should we send?
Anything fun: how they met, inside jokes, nicknames, embarrassing stories, dating disasters, favorite drinks, weird habits, exes worth mentioning — and anything the Cowboy absolutely should NOT mention. The juicier, the better the song.
Does the guest of honor know what you're going to sing?
No. That's the whole point. The dirt is submitted privately by the group and the guest of honor never sees any of it.
Can we request her favorite artist?
Yes — every package includes at least one song by her favorite artist, performed live on acoustic guitar, right after (or instead of) the original.
Do you do birthdays?
Birthdays are one of the most popular bookings. Milestone birthdays, surprise party openers, "she thinks we're just pregaming" — all of it.
Do you do divorce parties?
Tell the Cowboy about her ex. He'll write a song about him too. Divorce party songs are funny, cathartic and surprisingly popular.
How far in advance should we book?
The more notice, the better — 2–3 weeks is ideal so the song gets proper time. Last-minute requests are sometimes possible, so check availability.
Can we take photos and video?
Yes — and we encourage it. The reveal moment is the one you'll want on video. The Cowboy will pause for photos and hang around after the set.
What happens if our plans or time change?
We get it — groups run late in Nashville. Let us know as soon as possible and we'll do everything we can to adjust the surprise time.
Do you work with bachelorette party planners?
Yes — planners, concierge companies, hotels and party companies can offer Singing Cowboy Nashville as an add-on. See our partner page for wholesale and referral options.
