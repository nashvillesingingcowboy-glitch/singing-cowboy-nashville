The Nashville birthday surprise she'll actually remember

She thinks she's just pregaming. Then a cowboy walks in with a guitar — and the song he sings is about her.

The Moment

Another cake, or a song written about her life?

Milestone birthdays deserve better than another round of "Happy Birthday." A Singing Cowboy surprise turns the whole night into a story: her friends secretly send the details — the nickname, the disaster birthday from 2019, the thing she does when she's tipsy — and the Cowboy writes an original song and performs it live at the hotel, Airbnb or rooftop before the night out.

It works for 21sts, 30ths, 40ths, 50ths, retirements — any birthday where the person deserves to feel like the main character for five minutes.
